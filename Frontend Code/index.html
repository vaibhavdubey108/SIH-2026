The Gap

Expiry tracking exists. Expiry prevention doesn't.

Pharmacies and hospitals manage thousands of batches by hand. Near-expiry stock stays invisible until it's already waste — and today's inventory tools only tell you what already happened.

Honest Architecture

Not everything in MedSafe AI is AI — on purpose.

If a task can be solved with if expiry < today, we solve it that way. AI only earns its place where prediction, not comparison, is required.

DETERMINISTIC

Rule engine handles this

  • ⚙️ Store medicine, batch & stock in the database
  • ⚙️ Calculate daysRemaining = expiry − today
  • ⚙️ Compare expiry < today and flag it
  • ⚙️ Fire a reminder or notification
  • ⚙️ Block an already-expired batch from dispensing
  • ⚙️ Decode a barcode or QR value
PREDICTIVE

AI earns its place here

  • 🧠 Reading a messy, tilted, handwritten-style expiry stamp off real packaging
  • 🧠 Forecasting next month's demand from consumption history
  • 🧠 Scoring the probability a batch goes unused before it expires
  • 🧠 Flagging inventory or wastage that breaks from normal patterns
  • 🧠 Ranking which batch to move first across the whole stockroom
  • 🧠 Explaining *why* a batch was scored high-risk

What Ships In The Prototype

One platform, from the shelf to the disposal log.

📷

AI image scanning

Photograph any medicine box and let computer vision find the printed expiry stamp, however it's formatted.

🔤

OCR field extraction

Turns messy, tilted or blurry packaging text into clean medicine, batch and date fields.

🏷️

Barcode / QR verification

Cross-checks scanned text against barcode data for a second layer of confidence.

📈

Demand forecasting

Learns seasonal and branch-level consumption trends to predict next month's usage.

🧭

Expiry-risk prediction

Scores every batch on the probability it goes unused before its shelf life ends.

🔁

Smart FEFO recommendations

Tells staff exactly which batch to dispense first, ranked by real risk — not just date order.

🚫

Dispensing safety block

A deterministic rule engine — not the AI — refuses to let an already-expired batch go out.

⚠️

Anomaly detection

Flags inventory or wastage activity that breaks sharply from a medicine's usual pattern.

♻️

Disposal tracking

Keeps an auditable trail from "flagged high-risk" to "safely disposed."
